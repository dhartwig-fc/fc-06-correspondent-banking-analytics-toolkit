# Escalation and Case Management Framework

## Purpose

This document defines the operational lifecycle for Correspondent Banking alerts and investigations.

---

## Alert Lifecycle

```text
Alert Generated
        ↓
Level 1 Review
        ↓
Level 2 Investigation
        ↓
SME Assessment
        ↓
Decision
        ↓
Case Closure / SAR
```

---

## Level 1 Review

### Objectives

- Validate alert quality
- Confirm data availability
- Identify obvious false positives

### Outcomes

- Close Alert
- Escalate to Level 2

---

## Level 2 Investigation

### Objectives

- Conduct detailed account and transaction review
- Assess customer behaviour
- Review counterparties
- Analyse supporting documentation

### Outputs

- Investigation Summary
- Risk Assessment
- Recommendation

---

## SME Review

Required when:

- Complex correspondent/respondent banking structures exist
- Sanctions or PEP exposure identified
- Significant transaction values involved
- Regulatory concerns identified

Reviewers may include:

- Transaction Monitoring SME
- Financial Crime SME
- Sanctions Specialist

---

## Escalation Triggers

### High Priority

- Sanctions or PEP indicators
- Mule/funnel network indicators
- Cross-border layering indicators

### Medium Priority

- Significant volume/velocity anomalies
- Third-party payment activity
- Unusual account behaviour changes

### Low Priority

- Minor documentation inconsistencies

---

## SAR Escalation

Cases should be referred for SAR assessment where:

- Suspicion threshold met
- Criminal activity suspected
- Concealment behaviour identified

---

## Case Closure

Case closure should include:

- Investigation summary
- Findings
- Risk assessment
- Final decision
- Approvals

---

## Management Information

Recommended MI metrics:

- Alerts generated
- Alerts closed
- Escalation rate
- SAR referral rate
- Average investigation time
- False positive rate
