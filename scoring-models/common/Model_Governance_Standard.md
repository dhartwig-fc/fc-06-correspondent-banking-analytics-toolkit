# Correspondent Banking Analytics Model Governance Standard

## Purpose

This document defines governance requirements for Correspondent Banking Analytics risk models and scoring frameworks.

---

## Model Ownership

| Role | Responsibility |
|--------|--------|
| Financial Crime Analytics | Model design |
| Financial Crime Operations | Investigation feedback |
| Model Governance | Independent review |
| Compliance | Regulatory oversight |

---

## Review Frequency

| Activity | Frequency |
|------------|------------|
| Threshold review | Quarterly |
| Risk weighting review | Quarterly |
| Scenario effectiveness review | Semi-Annual |
| Full model validation | Annual |

---

## Performance Measures

- Alert volume
- False positive rate
- SAR conversion rate
- Escalation rate
- Investigator satisfaction

---

## Change Management

1. Change request raised
2. Impact assessment completed
3. Model validation performed
4. Governance approval obtained
5. Production deployment approved

---

## Retirement Criteria

A model should be retired when:

- Typology becomes obsolete
- Regulatory requirements change
- Superior model is available
- False positive rates become unacceptable
